The People Behind EDGE

Two operators who've run hotels from the inside — not just advised them.

Co-Founder & CEO

Thilanka Muthukumarana

A hospitality leader with more than 18 years of senior leadership experience across internationally recognized brands, including Hilton, Sonesta and Marriott in the United States, together with Waters Edge Colombo and Cinnamon Grand Colombo. His professional qualifications include a Hospitality Management Certificate from Cornell University, a bachelor's degree from Northwood, a business-focused university in the United States, and specialized sales training through Emirates hospitality schools Dubai.

  • 18+ years senior leadership
  • Hilton · Sonesta · Marriott (USA)
  • Waters Edge · Cinnamon Grand Colombo
  • Cornell University certificate

Group Managing Director

Rohan Fernandopulle

Nearly four decades of hospitality leadership, including 28 years with Hilton International, followed by senior executive roles at Waters Edge and the Galle Face Hotel Group. A pioneering Sri Lankan Executive Chef, President of Bocuse d'Or Sri Lanka, international culinary medalist and MasterChef Sri Lanka judge, he brings exceptional operational, culinary and leadership expertise to EDGE.

  • Nearly four decades
  • 28 years with Hilton International
  • President, Bocuse d'Or Sri Lanka
  • MasterChef Sri Lanka judge
